# Changelog

Notable changes to the public portfolio release are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — Portfolio release

- Structured gate-first bid qualification with separate attractiveness and winnability views.
- Commercial economics including probability of win, expected value and pursuit cost.
- Working-capital, retention/AR exposure and estimating/crew capacity checks.
- Sensitivity, robustness and beta-PERT Monte Carlo analysis.
- Decision records, overrides, pipeline outcomes and calibration reporting.
- Six synthetic sample bids covering the major decision states.
- One-page printable management decision memo.
- Local-first browser persistence, JSON import/export and explicit bid sharing.
- Unit-tested decision engine, standalone offline build, browser smoke testing and CI deployment.
- Documented methodology and architecture decisions.

The 1.0.0 label describes the stable public portfolio release. It does not imply production readiness for live commercial data.
